Romulo Nascimento

Product Designer · UX/UI · Research · Prototyping

I design products for people who handle complex work every day, such as claims analysts, staffing teams and laboratories.

I start with interviews and usability tests, and bring working prototypes to the conversation while the decision can still change.

I bring a collaborative approach to UX leadership, connecting different perspectives and helping teams turn research into design decisions.

At Porto Seguro, I redesigned a 40-year-old platform and brought claims analysis to about 25 minutes. At GFT, I helped cut bench cost by 72%. At Itaú, I turned the bank's motion language into an agent in Copilot.
