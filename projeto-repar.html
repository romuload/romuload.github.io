Conheça a REPAR 3D

A new version of Conheça a REPAR, originally created in Flash in 2007. The experience follows oil on its journey through the refinery.

The experience

A 3D model built in Blender presents the refinery's units. The camera guides visitors through ten stages, with questions and explanations about each part of the process.

Interaction and exploration

Pipes light up to reveal connections between units. Alongside the guided journey, exploration mode lets visitors navigate the model freely.
